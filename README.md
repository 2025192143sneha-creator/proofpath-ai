
# ProofPath — From Missing Pieces to Open Doors

### Evidence-First Scholarship Readiness Platform

ProofPath is a student-focused prototype designed to make scholarship preparation easier to understand and organize.

Students can use the dashboard to track document preparation, view a checklist completion score, identify items that need attention, and download a readiness report.

## The Problem

Scholarship applications can involve confusing eligibility criteria, multiple documents, and unclear application steps. Students may struggle to understand what they need to prepare.

## Our Solution

ProofPath brings scholarship preparation into one simple dashboard.

- **Document checklist:** Track documents you have prepared.
- **Readiness snapshot:** See your checklist completion percentage.
- **Personalized action plan:** Identify items that need attention.
- **Downloadable report:** Export your current checklist status.
- **Official resource link:** Access the National Scholarship Portal.

## How It Works

1. Enter your name.
2. Select a scholarship demo scenario.
3. Mark the documents you have prepared.
4. Analyze your readiness.
5. Review the suggested next steps.
6. Download your report.

## Technology Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- GitHub Pages for deployment

## Current Implementation

This version is a working frontend prototype. Its readiness score is calculated from the checklist items selected by the user.

The current version does not perform AI-based document verification, determine official eligibility, or connect to AWS services. These are potential future improvements.

## Future Scope

- Integrate AWS services for secure application data handling.
- Explore AI-powered guidance with evidence-linked explanations.
- Add verified scholarship information and eligibility checks.
- Improve privacy controls and accessibility.
- Test the platform with students and measure its usefulness.
